Formulas · 公式

N = stations × rain days × borrowings per station per rain day. Revenue = paid borrowings × fee + kept umbrellas × keep price + sponsorship × 12 + passes and orders × 12 + host fees received. Costs = site fees paid + stations × 12 × operating cost + payment fees (fixed fee per capture + rate × captured revenue) + (kept + lost) × umbrella cost. EBITDA = revenue − costs. Capex = stations × station cost + fleet × umbrella cost + app. Break-even sponsorship = (costs − all non-sponsor revenue) ÷ 12. Same expressions in build_business_brief.py.

年借出次数N = 站点数 × 雨天数 × 每站每雨天借出。收入 = 付费借出 × 费用 + 被留下的伞 × 买断价 + 赞助 × 12 + 月卡与订单 × 12 + 收到的场地费。成本 = 付出的场地费 + 站点 × 12 × 运营成本 + 支付手续费（每笔固定费 + 费率 × 扣款收入）+（留下 + 丢失）× 雨伞成本。EBITDA = 收入 − 成本。资本支出 = 站点 × 站点成本 + 伞队 × 雨伞成本 + App。盈亏平衡赞助 =（成本 − 所有非赞助收入）÷ 12。
