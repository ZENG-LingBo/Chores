Method: every direction in the holder's visual field (about 90,000 evenly spaced rays over the sphere, kept where they fall inside the field) is traced against the canopy's exact geometry, and counted as ad, plain fabric or open. Equal solid angle means equal weight. The umbrella shaft and the holder's arm aren't included in the count. All brands shown are fictional.
